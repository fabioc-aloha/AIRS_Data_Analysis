## Key Differentiators

### If your university has strict binding rules

Choose **DB Bookbinders / Thesis Binding USA**. They explicitly specialize in traditional thesis bindings (buckram, leather), which are commonly referenced in graduate school manuals.

### If you are up against a deadline

**Doxzoo** is the only provider that explicitly states **3-day production options**, including wire binding for drafts.

### If you want polished, modern presentation

**OnPress** focuses squarely on professional thesis books with hardcover and softcover options, positioned for submission and archival use.

### If your dissertation includes heavy visuals or custom specs

**Mixam** offers broad control over paper types, finishes, and layout, which is useful for analytics, design, or data-heavy dissertations.
